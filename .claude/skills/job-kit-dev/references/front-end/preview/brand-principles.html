Brand · key characteristics

Radius is 0

The whole Tailwind scale is aliased to it. Three deliberate exceptions only: sidebar rows, identifier chips, and avatars.

Borders replace shadows

Structure comes from a hairline at low ink opacity. Only the floating layer casts a shadow.

Ink is one hue, five opacities

Not a grey ramp — one base hue stepped down in light, mapping to warm off-whites in dark.

Three families, one job each

Inter for all UI, Space Mono for every identifier, Space Grotesk for the wordmark alone.

Neutral action color

Near-black drives every action. Semantic hues are muted and earthy; charts use a Tokyo-Night ramp.

Dark mode is true black

A true black canvas with lifted content — not a grey wash. Hairlines jump in opacity to survive the darker ground.

Density is deliberate

36px controls, 12px table headers, 53px rows, 14px body.
